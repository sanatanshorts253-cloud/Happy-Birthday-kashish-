Chapter 02

Just wanted to say…

READ THIS ONE SLOWLY

Hum log waise toh ek random GC mein mile the, aur us time humari koi khaas bonding bhi nahi thi. Main shayad bina sar-pair ki baatein karta tha, jaise mujhe karne ki aadat hai 😭😂 But tujhe lagta tha toh kya hi kar sakte hain… usko chhod. 😂

But honestly, time ke saath jab humne normal si cheezon par baat karna start kiya, pata hi nahi chala ki kab humari understanding itni achhi ban gayi.

Aaj tu meri life ka ek important part ban chuki hai. Tujhse main bina kisi hesitation ke kuch bhi share kar sakta hoon, kisi bhi situation ke baare mein discuss kar sakta hoon. Aur sabse achhi baat ye hai ki tu mujhe samajhti bhi hai aur samjhati bhi hai.

Maine tujhe bahut saari situations ke baare mein bataya hai aur tune hamesha apne hisaab se mujhe best advice dene ki koshish ki hai. Uske liye genuinely, thank you. ❤️

Itne kam time mein tu itni close ho gayi, ye honestly maine expect nahi kiya tha. Bas aise hi hamesha khush reh, hasti reh aur smile karti reh. 🫶🏻 Aur haan, life mein kitni bhi confusion ho, thodi kam confused rehna please. 😂

Bhale hi main teri life ka ek important part nahi hoon, but tu meri life ka ek bahut important part ban chuki hai. ❤️

Aur ek cheez yaad rakhna—main hamesha tere saath hoon. Raat ke kitne bhi baje ho, agar main soya nahi hoon, toh tu mujhe kabhi bhi text kar sakti hai.

Agar koi problem ho, kuch share karna ho, ya bas baat karni ho—I’m there.

Baaki, jo kuch bhi tune mere liye kiya hai, uske liye thank you so much. ❤️

Once again, Happy Birthday, Kashish! 🎂❤️

Stay happy. Stay smiling.
And trust the process. 🫶🏻
